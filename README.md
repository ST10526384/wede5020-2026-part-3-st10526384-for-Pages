## Project Overview
TDP which stands for Travelling Destination Provider is a South African travelling service that offers places for people to visit/see in South Africa. 
TDP gives information about the places people might want to go to or offer any assistance tofind places that suits their budget and as well as their interests. 
TDP is for people who want to find out about places that fit their budget and interests. This is the proposal I have chosen to make a website


## Website Goals and Objectives
The most important goal for TDP is to provide/offer assistance to people who are trying to find places to go in South Africa and plan their trips more easily and organised. TDP’s website is to offer useful information about places in the country that matches a person’s interests.
Below are other objectives for TDP:
-	Comparing different places to see which is more suitable or better for the person
-	Support the idea for people to explore different places in South Africa
-	Increasing the local travelling numbers
  
### Below are the following KPIs: 
-	The number of people that visits the site
-	The number of popular places that have been searched
- The number of people having enquires


## Key Features and Functionality:
The TDP website will have the following pages:
-	A homepage which is the main page everyone will see
-	An about us page that tells what TDP is and their goals 
-	A contact us page which gives a form and contact information 
-	Destination page that shows and gives about locations in South Africa
•	Destination vs Destination page where people can compare to two places with one another
•	Travelling assistance page that offers assistance and answers frequently asked questions
•	An enquiry page people can ask about travelling enquires or other information
The functionality of the website is people are able to get recommendations for places to travel and information about places.
