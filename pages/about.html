What TDP is all About

The organisation "Travelling Service Provider" TDP is South African local travelling service that offers to assist travelers to find the perfect and best locations in South Africa

TDP provides incredible information about locations, offers travelling assistance and allows you to compare and contrast two different places, making it easier to see which one is better for you

Mission Statement:

Our mission as an organisation is to be able to help those who are not from South Africa or people who want to travel and have an easy time to get any information about places

, find the perfect budget for the trip, compare two places or get any other asisstance if needed in order for them to have a great experience in South Africa

Vision Statement:

Our vision as an organisation is to be able support and uplift people in South Africa or across the world. Discover and explore South Africa as it can offer breath taking experiences,

activites and more

The People that We Wish to Help

The organisation TDP wants to help as many as possible travelers who are trying or wishing to explore/learn more of the different places in South Africa.

TDP also wishes to offer people any useful information about places and how they can plan their trips appropriately

  • South Africans who love to travel locally
  • Family or friends looking for a trip
  • People who are looking to travel somewhere with their current/limited budget
  • People who are not from South Africa wishing to experience wonderful places
